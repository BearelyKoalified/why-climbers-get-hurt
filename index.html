Before you quote a number

  • No denominator. These are accident counts, not accidents per climbing day. Nothing here shows one discipline is more dangerous than another.
  • Accidents carry several tags — 1.38 immediate causes and 0.95 contributory causes each — so shares never sum to 100%.
  • The cumulative columns are a 66-year aggregate weighted toward the mountaineering era. Hover any bar for its 2017 figure; the mix has shifted hard toward rappelling, lowering and belay errors.
